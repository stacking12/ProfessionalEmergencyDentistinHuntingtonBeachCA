Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about emergency dental care in Huntington Beach.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe or lasting toothache, a knocked-out, cracked or broken tooth, swelling in the gums or face, bleeding that won't stop, and a suspected abscess are all reasons to seek an emergency dental visit. If you're unsure, call us and we'll help you decide.

Can I get a same day dentist appointment?

Yes. We make room for urgent cases so you can be seen quickly. Call 657-301-3049 as early as you can and tell us your symptoms so we can plan your visit.

Do you offer after hours or 24 hour dentist care?

Yes. Our after hours and 24 hour dentist support is built for problems that can't wait until morning. Call us and we'll explain the fastest way to get treated.

What should I do while I wait to see the dentist?

Rinse gently with warm water, use a cold compress on the cheek to reduce swelling, and avoid very hot, cold or hard foods. If a tooth was knocked out, keep it moist and bring it with you. Don't place aspirin directly on the gums.

Is emergency dental care affordable?

We aim to keep emergency care affordable and transparent. We'll explain your treatment options and costs before we start, so you can decide with confidence. Ask us about payment and insurance questions when you call.
